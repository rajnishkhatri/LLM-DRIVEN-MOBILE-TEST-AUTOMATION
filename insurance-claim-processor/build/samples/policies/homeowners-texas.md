# Homeowners HO-3 — Texas (sample policy excerpt)

Form: HO3-TX-2023
Jurisdiction: Texas
Line of business: homeowners
Effective: 2025-06-01 through 2026-06-01

Sudden and accidental discharge of water from a plumbing system is
covered when the failure is hidden. Gradual seepage, flood, and
groundwater are excluded. Cabinet replacement is covered only when the
cabinets themselves are damaged by the covered water, not for matching
undamaged materials in adjoining rooms.
