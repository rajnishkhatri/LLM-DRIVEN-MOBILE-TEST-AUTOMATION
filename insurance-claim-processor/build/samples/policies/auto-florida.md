# Personal Auto — Florida (sample policy excerpt)

Form: PA-FL-2024
Jurisdiction: Florida
Line of business: personal auto
Effective: 2025-01-01 through 2026-12-31

Collision coverage pays for sudden, accidental damage to the insured
vehicle from impact with another vehicle or object, minus the deductible.
A police report is required when a third party is involved.

This form does not cover mechanical breakdown, wear, or damage that
occurs outside Florida unless the insured is traveling for fewer than
30 days.
