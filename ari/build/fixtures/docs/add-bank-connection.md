# Add a new bank connection

1. Go to **Settings -> Connections -> Banks**.
2. Click **Add connection** and choose your bank from the list.
3. Enter the credentials your bank issued for treasury access (never your
   personal login) and authorize the read-only scope.
4. Select which accounts to sync and click **Finish**. The first sync runs
   within a few minutes.

The connection settings live on the **Settings -> Connections -> Banks** page.
