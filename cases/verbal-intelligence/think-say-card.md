# The Think-Say Card

Say the answer. If you do not have it, say the test. One private pass a day, and the same three labels any time a problem is still open.

Memorize only: the governing sentence, the three labels, and the frame line. The traps and the four-minute rep stay on [The Tree Card](tree-card.md).

**Governing sentence:** "Say the answer. If you do not have it, say the test."

**Labels:** Piece. Sort. Say.

**Frame line:** "Give me a second. I want to frame this clearly."

## Before you answer

1. **Piece.** Name one behavior over time. Say what happened and where you can see it. "Late three times" is a piece. "I am bad at time" is not. If the name is a trait, cut it down until someone could watch it.
2. **Sort.** Walk in this order. Stop at the first move that fits.
   - **Chaotic.** Damage is spreading. Act. When it is stable, start at Piece again.
   - **Clear.** The move is obvious and it still works. That sentence is the trunk. If that step keeps failing, keep walking.
   - **The line.** Name the step that stays, the prize that protects it, and when the result arrives. Then one question: can someone find the cause, or only a try?
   - **Complicated.** Someone who knows can find the cause. Ask them. Split what you find so the parts do not overlap, they cover this piece, and they follow one order. Their summary is the trunk.
   - **Complex.** Only a try can show the cause. The trunk is one test you can afford to lose: what you will watch, the day you stop, and the sign that proves you wrong.
3. **Say.** Trunk first. The trunk is the insight, or the test. "There are three reasons" is the count, not the trunk. Then the count, at most three. Same kind. No overlap. Together they answer the question that was asked. One order: time, parts, or rank. Stop. Facts stay down until someone asks.

On the stop date, start at Piece again, smaller if the piece was too big. A question you cannot answer also starts at Piece.

## Once a day

Pick one open problem. Say the piece. Walk Sort and stop at the first move. Say the trunk, the count, and stop. Write the sentence you said, the sort you landed on, and the stop date.

If you named a trait, cut it to a behavior and start again. That restart is the rep.

## One pass

Late to meetings. The first pass has no answer, so the trunk is the test. The try shows the cause. The next pass is the same card on a smaller piece, and that pass is Clear.

| Blank | Fill |
|---|---|
| Piece | Late to meetings, a new excuse each time. |
| Chaotic | The meeting still happens. Keep walking. |
| Clear | A tight departure keeps failing. Keep walking. |
| Step that stays | A tight departure. |
| Prize | Arrive a few minutes early. |
| When you learn | At the door. |
| Cause, or only a try | Only a try. |
| What you watch | Arrival at the door. |
| Day you stop | The next time you reach the door. |
| Sign you are wrong | A tight departure already gets you there. |
| What the try showed | The cause. The next pass is Clear: leave a cushion. The piece is now the door. |
| Trunk | I leave a cushion, so I arrive a few minutes early. |
| Count | Two results. Most of the cushion gets used. Late stops. |

**Say this once:** "I leave a cushion, so I arrive a few minutes early. There are two results. Most of the cushion gets used. Late stops."

## Where the three names sit

- DART is Piece and Sort, then Piece again on the stop date.
- MECE is the Complicated split, and the check on the branches you say.
- [The Tree Card](tree-card.md) is Say.
